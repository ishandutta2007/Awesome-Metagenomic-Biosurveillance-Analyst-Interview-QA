# Glossary: Metagenomic Biosurveillance Analyst Terminology

## Terms A–M

**AMR (Antimicrobial Resistance)** – The ability of microorganisms to resist the effects of antimicrobial drugs; a major global public health threat tracked by resistome surveillance.

**Assembly (de novo)** – Computational reconstruction of a genome sequence from overlapping sequencing reads without a reference, enabling characterization of novel or highly-divergent organisms.

**CARD (Comprehensive Antibiotic Resistance Database)** – Curated AMR gene reference database used for resistome annotation; includes the Resistance Gene Identifier (RGI) tool.

**cgMLST (core genome MLST)** – Whole-genome typing approach using allele differences across hundreds to thousands of conserved core genes; higher discriminatory power than classical 7-gene MLST.

**CUSUM (Cumulative Sum)** – Statistical process control algorithm that accumulates deviations from baseline for sensitive detection of sustained trend changes in surveillance data streams.

**DIAMOND** – Fast protein-level sequence alignment tool enabling sensitive detection of divergent organisms by comparing translated reads against protein databases.

**DURC (Dual-Use Research of Concern)** – Research with legitimate scientific purpose that could be misused to pose a threat to public health or national security.

**EWMA (Exponentially Weighted Moving Average)** – Statistical process control method weighting recent observations more heavily, sensitive to recent trend changes in surveillance data.

**GISAID** – Global database for sharing influenza (and later SARS-CoV-2 and other pathogen) genome sequences; key international genomic surveillance data sharing platform.

**Host depletion** – Laboratory or computational methods for removing host-organism nucleic acids from metagenomic samples, enriching for pathogen/microbial sequences.

**IQ-TREE** – Maximum likelihood phylogenetic tree reconstruction software widely used for genomic epidemiology and novel pathogen characterization.

**Kraken2** – Fast k-mer-based taxonomic classification tool; the dominant read-level metagenomic classification approach for known pathogen surveillance.

**LCA (Lowest Common Ancestor)** – Taxonomic classification algorithm assigning reads to the most specific taxon consistent with all matching reference sequences; used in Kraken2 for ambiguous multi-match reads.

**MetaPhlAn** – Marker gene-based taxonomic profiling tool providing species-specific classification with low false positive rates.

**mNGS (metagenomic Next-Generation Sequencing)** – Unbiased sequencing of all nucleic acids in a clinical or environmental sample for pathogen-agnostic detection.

**MLST (Multi-Locus Sequence Typing)** – Bacterial typing system based on allele combinations at 7 conserved housekeeping genes; enables global comparison but limited outbreak discriminatory power.

## Terms N–Z

**One Health** – Collaborative framework recognizing the interconnection of human, animal, and environmental health; guides zoonotic biosurveillance program design.

**ONT (Oxford Nanopore Technology)** – Long-read, real-time sequencing platform enabling field-deployable same-day sequencing for acute outbreak response.

**PABS (Pathogen Access and Benefit Sharing)** – WHO framework governing equitable access to pathogen genetic resources and fair sharing of benefits from surveillance findings.

**Resistome** – The complete collection of antibiotic resistance genes in a microbial community; quantified by AMR metagenomic surveillance.

**RGI (Resistance Gene Identifier)** – CARD's software tool for AMR gene identification from genomic or metagenomic sequence data.

**RdRp (RNA-dependent RNA Polymerase)** – Key enzyme for RNA virus replication; conserved RdRp sequences used for phylogenetic placement of novel RNA viruses.

**Select Agent** – Pathogen or toxin designated by the CDC/USDA as posing severe threats to public health; subject to special regulatory requirements and reporting obligations.

**SNP (Single Nucleotide Polymorphism)** – Single base-pair difference between sequences; SNP-distance matrices are the primary measure of genomic relatedness in outbreak investigation.

**SPC (Statistical Process Control)** – Quality-control-derived statistical methods for detecting anomalies in longitudinal data streams; CUSUM and EWMA are primary SPC tools in surveillance.

**WBE (Wastewater-Based Epidemiology)** – Population-level surveillance using detection and quantification of pathogen markers (RNA, DNA, proteins) in wastewater.

**WHO GOARN (Global Outbreak Alert and Response Network)** – WHO's operational framework for international response to public health emergencies.
